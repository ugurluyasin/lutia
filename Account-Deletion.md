# Privacy Policy for Lutia

**Last updated:** 30 August 2026

Lutia ("the app") is developed by PeraLabs. This policy explains what the app
does and does not collect, and how the data it handles is used.

Lutia is designed to keep as much as possible on your device. Your training
plan, your workout history, and your original photos never leave your phone.

## No account required

Lutia does not require you to create an account. There is no sign-up, no
password, and we never ask for your name, email address, or phone number.

## What stays on your device

The following is stored **only** in the app's private storage on your phone and
is never transmitted to us:

- **Your original photos.** Every photo you take or select, in full, including
  both the originals and any crops you do not approve.
- **Your training profile.** Goals, experience level, available equipment,
  session frequency and duration, and any exercise exclusions or discomfort you
  record.
- **Your training history.** Workouts, sets, reps, loads, skipped exercises and
  their reasons, and effort feedback.
- **App usage events.** Records such as "workout started" or "check-in
  completed", stored in a local database to make the app work correctly. These
  are never uploaded.

## What is sent off your device

**One thing, and only if you choose to use the optional scan feature: the two
cropped images you explicitly approve.**

If you use the visual baseline or check-in:

1. You take or select two photos.
2. **You choose the exact crop of each one** before anything is sent.
3. Only those two approved crops are transmitted, once, for analysis.
4. We return a derived result — descriptive measurements, not an image.

Your original, uncropped photos are never transmitted. The crops are used only
to produce that single result. They are not used to train models and are not
used to build a profile of you.

The scan is entirely optional. Lutia's training features work fully without it.

## Third-party services

| Service | Purpose | What it receives |
|---|---|---|
| **Supabase** | Hosts the analysis function | The two approved crops, at the moment you request analysis |
| **OpenRouter** | Provides the model that analyses the crops | The two approved crops, for that single request |
| **RevenueCat** | Manages subscriptions | An anonymous identifier and your subscription status. No name, email, or payment details. |
| **Apple App Store / Google Play** | Process payments | Your payment information, handled entirely by Apple or Google. We never see or store your payment details. |

## Deleting your data

You are in control of everything the app holds:

- **Delete a scan and its photos** at any time from within the app.
- **Uninstall the app** to permanently remove all local data, including every
  photo, your profile, and your entire training history.

Because Lutia has no accounts and no user database, there is no server-side
profile to request the deletion of.

## Subscriptions

Lutia offers auto-renewing subscriptions. Payment is charged to your Apple or
Google account at confirmation of purchase. Subscriptions renew automatically
unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. You can
manage or cancel a subscription in your App Store or Google Play account
settings.

We never receive or store your payment card details. Purchases are verified
through RevenueCat using an anonymous identifier that is not linked to your
identity.

## Children

Lutia is intended for adults and is not directed to children. We do not
knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. Before using the scan
feature you confirm that you are 18 or older and that you are the person shown
in the photos.

## Health disclaimer

Lutia provides general fitness information and is not medical advice. It does
not diagnose, treat, or prevent any condition. Consult a qualified professional
before starting any exercise programme, particularly if you have an injury or a
medical condition.
