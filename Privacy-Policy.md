# Privacy Policy for Lutia

**Last updated:** 30 August 2026

Lutia ("the app") is developed by PeraLabs. This policy explains what the app
does and does not collect, and how the data it handles is used.

We designed Lutia to keep as much as possible on your device. Most of the app —
including your training plan, your workout history, and your photos — never
leaves your phone.

## No account required

Lutia does not require you to create an account. There is no sign-up, no
password, and we do not ask for your name, email address, or phone number.

## What stays on your device

The following are stored **only** in the app's private storage on your phone and
are never transmitted to us:

- **Body photos.** Photos you take or choose for a visual baseline or check-in,
  including the originals.
- **Your training profile.** Goals, experience level, available equipment,
  session frequency and duration, and any exercise exclusions or discomfort you
  record.
- **Your training history.** Workouts, sets, reps, loads, skipped exercises and
  their reasons, and effort feedback.
- **App usage events.** Records such as "workout started" or "check-in
  completed", kept locally to make the app work correctly.

Uninstalling the app deletes all of this. You can also delete individual scans
and their photos from within the app at any time.

## What is sent off your device

**Only one thing leaves your phone: the two cropped images you explicitly
approve for scan analysis.**

If you choose to use the optional visual baseline or check-in feature:

1. You take or select two photos.
2. **You choose the exact crop** of each one before anything is sent.
3. Only those two approved crops are transmitted, once, to our analysis service
   for processing.
4. We receive back a derived result — descriptive measurements, not an image.

Your original, uncropped photos are never transmitted. The crops are used only
to produce that single result and are not used to train models or build a
profile of you.

The scan feature is entirely optional. You can use Lutia's training features
without ever taking a photo.

## Third-party services

| Service | Purpose | What it receives |
|---|---|---|
| **Supabase** | Hosts the scan analysis function | The two approved image crops, at the moment you request analysis |
| **OpenRouter** | Provides the model that analyses the crops | The two approved image crops, for that single request |
| **RevenueCat** | Manages subscriptions | An anonymous identifier and your subscription status. No name, email, or payment details. |
| **Apple App Store / Google Play** | Process payments | Your payment information, handled entirely by Apple or Google. **We never see or store your payment details.** |

## Subscriptions

Lutia offers auto-renewing subscriptions. Payment is charged to your Apple or
Google account at confirmation of purchase. Subscriptions renew automatically
unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. You can
manage or cancel your subscription in your App Store or Google Play account
settings.

We do not receive or store your payment card details. Purchases are verified
through RevenueCat using an anonymous identifier that is not linked to your
identity.

## Children

Lutia is not directed to children and is intended for adults. We do not
knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. Before using the scan
feature you confirm that you are 18 or older and that you are the person shown
in the photos.

## Your choices

- **Skip the scan.** The training features work fully without it.
- **Delete your photos.** Remove any scan and its photos from within the app.
- **Delete everything.** Uninstalling the app removes all local data, including
  photos, your profile, and your training history.

## Health disclaimer

Lutia provides general fitness information and is not medical advice. It does
not diagnose, treat, or prevent any condition. Consult a qualified professional
before starting any exercise programme, particularly if you have an injury or a
medical condition.
